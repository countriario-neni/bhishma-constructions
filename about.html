Bhishma Constructions began in 2009 with a single duplex in Gorantla and a promise the client thought was unusual at the time: a fixed rate, written down, that would not move once the foundation was poured. It didn't move. He referred us to his brother, and that is more or less how the next sixteen years went.

Today we run apartment projects, villas, commercial floors and approved layouts across Guntur — but the operating model has not changed. Our engineers are on our payroll, not sub-contracted. Our supervisors are on site daily, not managing four projects from a phone. Concrete cubes go to the lab at 7 and 28 days whether or not anyone asks to see the report.

We are not the cheapest quote you will receive in Guntur. Builders who quote below us are usually planning to make the difference back at the finishing stage, once you have paid too much to walk away. We would rather show you the arithmetic upfront and let you decide.

Of the 240 projects we have delivered, 98% were handed over on or before the promised date. The families in the other 2% were told the moment we knew, and told why. That is the whole business, honestly — no abandoned sites, no disappearing acts, and a phone number that still connects to the same people after the cheque clears.
