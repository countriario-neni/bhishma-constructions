Straight Answers

Questions We Get Every Week

Will the price change halfway through the build?

No. The per-square-foot rate is signed before we break ground and it covers the specification in your agreement. The only time a figure moves is if you change the scope — a bigger balcony, an imported fitting, an extra room. Those are quoted and approved by you in writing first. "Steel rates went up" is our problem, not yours.

How do I know what materials are actually going into my house?

Every client gets a WhatsApp group with our site supervisor, the project engineer and you. Material invoices are posted to it as they are purchased — cement, TMT steel, sand, tiles, sanitaryware. Concrete cubes are tested at 7 and 28 days and the lab reports are shared in the same group.

How long does a typical independent house take?

A 2,000–2,500 sq.ft. duplex on a clear plot runs 9 to 11 months from foundation to handover. Approvals add roughly 4 to 6 weeks before that, which we run in parallel with your design finalisation wherever the sanction allows.

I'm an NRI and can't visit the site. Can you still work with me?

Yes — a good share of our villa clients are in the Gulf, the US and Australia. You get weekly video walkthroughs, dated site photos, material bills and a scheduled call with the project engineer. Payments are staged against completed work, so you are never paying ahead of what you can see.

What happens after handover if something goes wrong?

You get a 10-year structural warranty and one free year of snag rectification — leaks, hairline cracks, door alignment, electrical faults. Call the same number you have been calling all along. We do not hand you off to a different department after the cheque clears.

Do you help with the home loan?

We prepare and submit the builder-side documentation — approved plan, title documents, RERA registration, cost estimate and construction schedule — in the format SBI, HDFC and LIC Housing ask for. Clients on our RERA-registered projects typically get sanction in 9 to 15 days.
